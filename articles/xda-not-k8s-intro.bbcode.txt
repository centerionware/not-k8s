[HEADING=1]not-k8s: replaced kubelet with a 15MB Rust agent[/HEADING]

A k3s control plane (apiserver, scheduler, full kubectl/CRD support) with kubelet swapped out for [B]nodelet[/B], a leaner event-driven agent written in Rust.

Benchmarked against upstream kubelet, both completely idle, same control plane, six separate runs so the numbers couldn't be biased by testing back to back on an already-warmed-up box:

[LIST]
[*][B]Memory[/B] — ~15MB nodelet vs ~81MB kubelet
[*][B]CPU (2min idle)[/B] — ~0.08s nodelet vs ~0.85s kubelet
[/LIST]
