[HEADING=1]not-k8s: replaced kubelet with a 15MB Rust agent, profiled on a Pixel 7[/HEADING]

[B]nodelet[/B] is a drop-in replacement for kubelet, written in Rust, event-driven instead of polling. Same control plane, same kubectl, same CRDs.

Idle, no pods scheduled, 120s window, 3 replicates per agent:

[LIST]
[*][B]x86_64[/B] — nodelet ~15MB / ~0.08s CPU, kubelet ~81MB / ~0.85s CPU
[*][B]Pixel 7 (Debian VM)[/B] — nodelet 12.0MB / 0.436s CPU, kubelet 67.9MB / 8.031s CPU
[/LIST]

The CPU gap widens from 10.6x to 18.4x on the slower core; memory stays flat at ~5.5x. Could be cache, could be the in-order A55, could be Go's GC — can't tell, the PMU isn't exposed to the VM so perf gives no hardware counters.

On that phone the k3s control plane idles at ~34% of a core and ~350MB, more than either agent.
