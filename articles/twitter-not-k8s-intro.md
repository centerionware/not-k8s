kubelet idles at ~81MB RAM / ~0.85s CPU (2min idle) per Kubernetes node — real energy either way, from constant polling that scans/rewrites memory. nodelet, a 15MB Rust replacement, idles at ~15MB / ~0.08s: ~5x/~10x less. At 1,000 nodes: ~66GB more schedulable RAM, ~$400/mo at Fargate on-demand rates. Open source: https://github.com/centerionware/not-k8s
